# Core research analysis

The project asks whether shared-core `high_overlap` instances make Greedy
miss the optimum more often than a `uniform` control matched on dimensions and
expected set size. The comparison concerns the generating mechanisms;
matching does not isolate overlap from every other structural difference.

The [completed fixed pilot](overlap_pilot_v1.md) did not provide sufficient
paired evidence of a failure-rate difference at its prescribed setting.
[C1](../experiments/core_rq/CLAIMS.md#c1) links the observation, diagnostic
table and figure to the frozen evidence and validation record.

Use the [pilot commands](../docs/cli.md#core-overlap-pilot) to reproduce the work
or the [original plan](../docs/core_overlap_checkpoint_plan.zh-CN.md) to inspect
its design and stopping conditions. The [documentation index](../docs/README.md)
covers method checks, examples and broader exploration.

Evidence publication requirements are maintained in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#evidence-backed-research-claims).
